# MIT KEMAR head-related impulse responses

`mit_kemar_normal_pinna.sofa` is derived from the MIT KEMAR HRTF measurements by Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, MIT Media Laboratory. The dataset is copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory. The authors provide it free of charge without use restrictions, provided they are cited when the data is used in research or commercial applications.

Citation: Gardner, W. G., and Martin, K. D. (1995), “HRTF measurements of a KEMAR,” Journal of the Acoustical Society of America 97, 3907–3908.

Original dataset and terms: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html

SOFA conversion: Piotr Majdak and Michael Mihocic, Acoustics Research Institute, Austrian Academy of Sciences. Source: https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/mit/mit_kemar_normal_pinna.sofa
